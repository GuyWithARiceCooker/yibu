Measures a short camera clip and tries to transcribe it. Test page, not part of Yibu.

  2. In the camera, record about 10 seconds of clear speech (any language). Hold the iPad about an arm's length away.
  3. Tap Use Video. Wait for iOS to finish preparing it.
  4. Check the file size below, then tap Upload and transcribe.
